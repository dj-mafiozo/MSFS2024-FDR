Charts

Choose parameters and their order in chart settings. Adjacent samples are connected with straight lines.

Drag to pan. Shift+drag selects a zoom range, Ctrl+wheel zooms, and the wheel scrolls charts. Full flight resets the range. The return button in a detached window restores the panel to the main window.

Parameter labels are readable and include known units. Search by label or raw ID; tooltips show the stored identifier.
