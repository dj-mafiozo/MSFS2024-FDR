B38M PFD: FMA, guidance and flight information

The three FMA fields above attitude separate thrust, lateral and vertical guidance. For iFly their contents are reconstructed from recorded AFDS flags, MCP targets and standard data. Armed is not active. In older flights without SDK data, a standard AUTOPILOT MASTER zero does not prove CMD was off.

FD bars use recorded pitch/bank commands, not actual motion. IAS/Mach and BARO/STD follow recorded selections. BARO/RADIO minima require an available selected value; a tape marker needs the appropriate altitude reference. Optional AoA also requires valid data.

The side panel shows flight number, zero-padded XPDR, SELCAL, registration, recorded UTC/date and elapsed time. Debug settings hide it or choose CPT left/FO right. The wind arrow is heading-relative. VOR/ADF blocks beside heading show recorded sources, identifiers, tuning and available DME.
