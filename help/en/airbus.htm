A320 PFD and upper/lower ECAM

Fenix and FBW support depends on addon/version and the data captured.

Option / actionBehavior and limitations
PFDRecorded attitude, tapes, heading, FMA, FD, ILS and barometric/speed modes; supported FBW ARINC validity is respected.
MinimaBARO, RADIO and NO DH when available. RADIO is relative to radio height, not an arbitrary MSL marker. Missing pilot settings are not invented.
E/WDAvailable N1, EGT, N2, fuel flow/quantity, configuration, warning and memo text. Missing state is not an all-clear cockpit.
RecordedThe lower ECAM follows the page recorded at the cursor. Without page-state telemetry, the original page cannot be established reliably.
Manual pagesENG, BLEED, PRESS, ELEC, HYD, FUEL, APU, COND, DOOR, WHEEL, F/CTL, STS, CRUISE inspect the chosen page at current replay time. They send no aircraft command.
Warnings / STSReconstructed from supported recorded flags/catalogs, not a pixel capture or a guarantee of every proprietary warning.
