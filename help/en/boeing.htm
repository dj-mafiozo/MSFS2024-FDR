B38M PFD, EICAS and flight information

Dedicated MAX frames are selected for B38M. This is not complete B736–B739 avionics emulation.

Option / actionBehavior and limitations
PFDRecorded IAS/Mach/altitude tapes, attitude, heading, VSI, selected targets, barometric setting/STD, ILS, radio altitude and available minima.
InformationShows flight number, squawk including leading zeros, SELCAL, registration, recorded UTC/date and elapsed time. CPT is left, FO right. Hide it in Debug settings.
Flight DirectorValid recorded standard pitch/bank commands drive the crossbars. If the addon publishes no meaningful commands, FD cannot be reconstructed reliably; disable it in settings.
FMAiFly FMA is reconstructed from SDK/MCP states and standard data, and labelled as reconstructed. Missing historical SDK data are not authoritative OFF states. VNAV/flare/thrust transitions can be approximate.
Wind / VOR / ADFWind arrow is relative to heading; 1/2 blocks show tuning and available DME. An unavailable station is not a station at zero distance.
AoA / minimaOptional AoA uses recorded incidence and ground inhibit. Minima require available pilot-setting data; design decision-height constants are not selected minima.
EICASEngine, fuel, flap and available annunciator data follow the recording. Old flights may have blank fields.
