About

MSFS2024-FDR is a native Delphi 10.2 VCL Windows application.

Originally based on MichaelHsu170/MSFS-Flight-Data-Recorder (Qt5), AGPL-3.0. Architecture and features have since been extensively rebuilt. Attribution and dependency licenses remain documented in NOTICE.en.md.
