A320 upper ECAM: E/WD

The upper display combines both engines’ N1, EGT, N2 and fuel flow with FOB, flap configuration and message areas. N1/EGT have arcs and numeric readings; N2/FF help compare engines. FOB is remaining fuel, not accumulated fuel used.

Warnings and memos are reconstructed from supported flags and catalogs, not captured pixels of the original ECAM. Messages depend on data and flight phase. Older files lacking relevant flags cannot prove an absence of faults. E/WD follows replay time regardless of the manually selected lower page.

Interface illustration with synthetic demonstration data; not a real flight recording.
