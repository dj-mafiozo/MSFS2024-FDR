FMC/MCDU replay

Recorded display content and available annunciators follow the timeline. The frame is not remote control of a live cockpit.

Option / actionBehavior and limitations
DeviceAircraft data infer A320/B737/available B777 format; no manual device selector is needed.
Left / rightSelect the recorded unit's tab. Units may have different pages/timestamps; missing data on one side are not inferred from the other.
FormattingAvailable colors, size/style and controls retain the appropriate device background. Positional telemetry alone cannot recover missing screen history.
SeekingBackward seek uses the last screen observed by that time. Long unchanged pages can be valid; screen updates may be recorded only on change.
DetachWindow action and docking preserve time synchronization.
