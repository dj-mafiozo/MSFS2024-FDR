Playback and recorded time

At 1x, time follows the recording rather than advancing one sample per timer tick. Interpolation smooths continuous values and angles while switches retain discrete transitions. Pause to select an instant with the slider or chart. Stop returns to the beginning and 1x. Normal replay jumps are 30 seconds; separate ghost actions jump 10 seconds.

Normal playback controls the analysis timeline, not a simulator aircraft. Ghost replay is separate.

Option / actionBehavior and limitations
Play / pauseRuns or pauses the selected flight; instruments, map, charts, FMC and state/message tabs follow the same recorded cursor.
StopStops, returns to the beginning and resets rate to 1x.
Beginning / endJump to the first/last point. Back/Skip jump 30 seconds within recording bounds.
RateSelect 0.125, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16x. The rate label shows the choice. Faster playback cannot add missing samples.
Slider / hoverSeek directly with the timeline. Active playback disables chart-hover seeking; pause before inspecting time with the mouse.
InterpolationContinuous values/angles can interpolate. Switches and SDK modes remain discrete; gaps and absent values are not valid zeroes.
ATC captionThe last eligible line appears near the slider for about 30 recorded seconds. Rewinding shows historical context, not the latest live message.
