Aircraft checklists

The Checklists tab displays checklists exposed by the simulator through the optional MSFS24-FDR-Bridge. This is a read-only view and does not complete or reset cockpit checklists.

Option / actionBehavior and limitations
SelectionUse the dropdown at the top to choose a checklist. Its items, expected settings and available completion states appear below.
Item stateChecked means completed according to simulator data. Unchecked means a recorded incomplete state; gray means the state is unknown. Missing data does not prove that a procedure was not performed.
ReplayChanges are stored with recording time. Seeking restores the latest state known at that time and never shows future completion early. A manually selected checklist remains selected while it exists.
AvailabilityThe bridge must run during recording. Add-on proprietary checklists may not be exposed by the native listener. Older recordings without checklist data display an empty-state message.
WindowDetach and dock this tab like other data tabs. Selecting or scrolling a checklist sends no commands to the aircraft.
