Turboprop engines

Torque measures shaft load, prop RPM the propeller speed, and Ng/N1 the gas generator speed. These are different quantities: propeller RPM is not N1. ITT helps inspect starts and power changes. Oil pressure/temperature and fuel flow remain alongside them.

Compare torque changes with recorded condition lever and beta/reverse states when available. Low airspeed alone does not prove reverse. Read the unit on each instrument. A Debug layout override cannot turn missing jet telemetry into turboprop measurements.
