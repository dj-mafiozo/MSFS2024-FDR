Engine gauges and configuration

The generic panel follows engine type/count. Dedicated B38M EICAS/A320 ECAM replaces it for those display kinds.

Option / actionBehavior and limitations
PistonAvailable RPM, EGT/CHT, oil pressure/temperature, left/right fuel, electrical/vacuum readings and condition/reverse state.
TurbopropTorque, prop RPM, ITT, Ng/N1 gas generator, oil, fuel flow and available condition/beta/reverse states.
JetN1, N2, EGT, fuel flow, oil and available vibration. Thrust/autothrottle state supplements rather than replaces measured engine data.
UnitsOil pressure uses psi rather than psf. Temperature, flow and quantity follow their defined/configured units. Missing readings stay unavailable.
WindowDetach, resize proportionally and dock back. The scaled panel is not a scrollable cropped instrument.
